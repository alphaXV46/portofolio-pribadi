## Problem Statement

Distribution companies handle high transaction volumes daily, requiring reliable stock allocation tracking, instant PDF invoice dispatching, and automated financial report generation for multi-warehouse management.

## Technical Decisions & Solutions

- **Laravel 13 Architecture**: Designed robust relational data models in MySQL managing multi-tier pricing, customer credits, and warehouse stock movements.
- **Livewire Reactive Tables**: Built interactive real-time data tables for instant search, filtering, and stock adjustments without full page reloads.
- **DomPDF & Maatwebsite Excel**: Streamlined document generation for instant PDF invoice rendering and bulk Excel exporting for accounting audits.

## Key Challenges & Results

Eliminated manual invoicing bottlenecks and accelerated monthly inventory reconciliation.
