## Problem Statement

Fitness enthusiasts need real-time workout stats without relying on continuous internet connectivity during gym workouts.

## Technical Decisions & Solutions

- **Offline-First Storage**: Integrated WatermelonDB with local SQLite for seamless offline state persistence.
- **BLE Integration**: Built native bridges for Bluetooth Low Energy heart rate monitors.

## Key Challenges & Results

Over 10,000 active app installs with seamless local syncing when reconnecting to Wi-Fi.
